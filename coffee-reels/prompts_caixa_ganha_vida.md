# Série "A caixa ganha vida": próximos episódios (prompts para o Gemini / Veo)

Como usar: modo **Vídeos**, formato EM PÉ (9:16, botão do retângulo embaixo da caixa de texto), anexar a imagem
`out/N_<jogo>_9x16.png` (refeita em 11/nov: caixa 900 px no degradê creme + sombra) e colar o prompt. Sem texto no vídeo.

1. **Wingspan** — The box cover comes to life: the painted bird on the cover slowly flaps its wings, lifts off the
   cover and flies out of the box into a cozy real living room, gliding across the room and landing gently on a wooden
   bookshelf full of board games. Soft morning light, gentle birdsong and wing flaps. Smooth camera, realistic, no text,
   vertical 9:16.
2. **Ticket to Ride** — The box cover comes to life: steam puffs from the old locomotive on the cover, the train
   whistles and rolls out of the box onto a real wooden table, running along a colorful railway map with little plastic
   train cars until it stops beside the box. Warm afternoon light, train whistle and wheels on rails. Realistic, smooth
   camera, no text, vertical 9:16.
3. **Draftosaurus** — The box cover comes to life: the cartoon dinosaurs on the cover blink, wiggle and hop out of the box
   as small wooden dinosaur meeples, running around a tiny playground on a real wooden table (slide, sandbox), playful and
   cute. Bright daylight, happy little stomps and squeaks. No text, vertical 9:16.
4. **Dixit** — The box cover comes to life: the dreamlike scene on the cover starts moving, large illustrated cards float
   out of the box like magical doors, drifting through the air into a cozy café with colorful paper umbrellas hanging
   from the ceiling, and land softly on a wooden café table. Warm café ambience, soft magical chimes. Dreamy, smooth camera,
   no text, vertical 9:16.
5. **Flamecraft** — The box cover comes to life: the little red dragon on the cover yawns, flies out of the box and
   breathes small warm flames that light up tiny lanterns and shop windows of a miniature fantasy town on a real wooden
   table, one by one, at dusk. Cozy glow, crackle of fire, soft fantasy music. No text, vertical 9:16.

Ao receber cada clipe: `brutos/geminiN.mp4` + entrada em `EPS` de `becas-reels/serie_caixa.py` (conferir se veio deitado).
O Dixit fecha com o convite da tarde no Beca’s (como `becas-reels/cafe_rush.py`).
